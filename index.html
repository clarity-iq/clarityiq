Before you begin

You're talking with an AI.

ClarityIQ uses an AI voice assistant, not a human, to walk you through cataract and lens education. It's built to give you accurate, general information — not medical advice, and not a substitute for your surgeon. Nothing it tells you is a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation specific to you. Your actual care decisions happen with your eye doctor, based on your own eyes and history.

ClarityIQ is an educational tool only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or clinical decision support. The information provided is general in nature and may not apply to your specific situation. Always consult a qualified eye care professional before making any decisions about your eye care or surgery. Use of ClarityIQ does not create a doctor-patient relationship between you and ClarityIQ, DLP Ventures, LLC, or any individual associated with it.

Your conversation is processed in real time solely to generate a personalized educational summary. The summary is shown to you and emailed once. We do not store the conversation, the summary, or any related content after the session ends.
